Under the title “Healing the Middle East – A Renewed Paradigm for a Shared Future” , Rabbi Dr. Yakov Nagen will share a vision for a model of peace and understanding in the Middle East, rooted in religious fraternity and mutual respect. Drawing on his experience as Director of the Blickle Institute for Interfaith Dialogue, Rabbi Nagen will explore how spiritual and interreligious approaches can help bridge divides between faith communities and inspire cooperation across borders. His reflections highlight the transformative role of religion—not as a source of conflict, but as a force for reconciliation and shared humanity. After his talk, participants will have the opportunity to join an open conversation with Rabbi Nagen.
